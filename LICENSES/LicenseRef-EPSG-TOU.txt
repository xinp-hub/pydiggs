src/pydiggs/crs/epsg-subset.json holds facts (name, type, axis order, unit, datum,
components) extracted unmodified from the EPSG Geodetic Parameter Dataset v12.029
(2025-10-02) with PROJ 9.8.1.

The EPSG Dataset is owned by the International Association of Oil & Gas Producers (IOGP)
and is used under the EPSG Dataset Terms of Use:

    https://epsg.org/terms-of-use.html

That page is the authoritative text; it is not reproduced here. Among other things the
terms require that IOGP's ownership of the dataset be acknowledged in any publication or
transmission of it, and that anyone to whom it is provided be told of the terms. This
file and the pyDIGGS NOTICE file do both. The full dataset is at https://epsg.org.

The extract is not covered by the pyDIGGS Apache-2.0 licence.
